I use video editing to transform interviews, documentary footage, and recorded moments into engaging visual stories.

Editing is more than putting clips together.

My approach to video editing focuses on storytelling, pacing, sound, and visual continuity. I enjoy finding the narrative within raw footage and using editing to guide the viewer through a story.

My experience includes documentary interviews, promotional content, archival projects, and multimedia work.
